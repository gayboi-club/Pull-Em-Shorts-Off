# Privacy Policy for Pull Em Shorts Off

**Last updated:** May 20, 2026

## Overview

Pull Em Shorts Off is a browser extension that helps users avoid endless scrolling on YouTube Shorts. This privacy policy explains what data the extension collects and how it is used.

## Data Collection

### What We Collect

The extension stores the following data **locally on your device only**:

- **User preferences**: Toggle states for various features (blocking scroll, hiding sidebar, etc.)
- **Usage statistics**: Your daily scroll block count and daily streak (kept locally)
- **Custom GIF URL**: If you set a custom replacement image

### What We Do NOT Collect

- We do not collect any personal information
- We do not track your browsing history
- We do not send any data to external servers
- We do not use analytics or tracking

## Data Storage

All data is stored locally using the browser's built-in storage API (`browser.storage.local`). This data never leaves your device.

## Third-Party Services

- **YouTube**: The extension runs on YouTube pages to block Shorts scrolling
- **Tenor**: Default GIF is loaded from Tenor (you can change or disable this)

## Your Rights

You can:
- Disable or uninstall the extension at any time
- Clear all stored data by removing the extension

## Changes

This privacy policy may be updated. Any changes will be reflected in the extension update.
